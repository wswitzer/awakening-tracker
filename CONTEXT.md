# Domain Context

## Awakening hypothesis
A falsifiable research hypothesis that populations may be changing over time in observable directions associated with self-transcendence, interconnectedness, compassion, contemplative awareness, meaning orientation, and reduced othering. It is not a presumption and is not itself directly measurable.

## Construct
A defined psychological, behavioral, cultural, or linguistic concept the project attempts to measure, such as generalized trust, forgiveness orientation, or self-transcendence.

## Observation
A value or text span obtained directly from a source before project-specific transformation.

## Derived measure
A value produced from one or more observations through a documented transformation.

## Interpretation
A claim about what observations or derived measures may mean. Interpretations must remain distinguishable from measurements.

## Source
An identifiable dataset, publication, API, corpus, or primary document from which observations or factual claims originate.

## Provenance
The machine-resolvable chain connecting a claim or result to its source, versions, transformations, and execution history.

## Claim
A substantive assertion that requires evidentiary support.

## Protocol
A versioned set of predetermined rules governing an analysis or experiment, including hypotheses, data selection, exclusions, variables, transformations, and statistical tests.

## Frozen protocol
A protocol that has been preregistered or otherwise locked for confirmatory execution and may not be mutated in place.

## Exploratory analysis
Analysis in which methods may legitimately evolve after inspecting data. Its results generate or refine hypotheses rather than count as preregistered confirmation.

## Confirmatory analysis
Analysis executed against a frozen protocol and protected outcome data in order to test a predetermined hypothesis.

## Development set
Data intentionally available while developing methods, classifiers, and harness behavior.

## Validation set
Data used to choose among candidate methods or harness changes after development.

## Sealed set
Data protected from the method-development loop until a preregistered confirmatory execution is authorized.

## Run
One reproducible execution of a specific protocol version against identified input data and code versions.

## Finding
A result emitted by a run, including uncertainty and provenance.

## Pathology
A recurring or generalizable failure mode discovered through execution, verification, review, or reflection.

## Heuristic
A reusable candidate rule intended to reduce a known pathology. Heuristics are working memory, not constitutional rules.

## Eval
An executable or objectively gradable case used to evaluate an agent, classifier, workflow, or harness behavior.

## Harness patch
A proposed modification to agent workflow, prompts, routing, verification, memory, or orchestration.

## Promotion
Acceptance of a harness patch after its candidate behavior beats or safely matches the current harness under the required evaluation gates.

## Skeptic
An adversarial research role tasked with finding plausible non-awakening explanations, methodological weaknesses, and disconfirming evidence. The Skeptic does not decide the final conclusion.

## Fresh-context audit
An independent milestone review performed without initially inheriting the prior agent's interpretive conclusions.
