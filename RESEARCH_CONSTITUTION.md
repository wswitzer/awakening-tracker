# Research Constitution

This file defines non-negotiable scientific constraints for the Awakening Tracker. Autonomous agents may read and apply this constitution but may not silently weaken, reinterpret, or rewrite it.

## Core principles

1. **Neutral hypothesis posture** — The project does not presume that spiritual awakening is increasing, decreasing, or occurring at all.
2. **Equal status for counterevidence** — Evidence against the awakening hypothesis must be retained, analyzed, and presented with the same care as supportive evidence.
3. **No outcome-driven construct drift** — Constructs, weights, inclusion rules, exclusions, and definitions may not be changed because an observed result is inconvenient or desirable.
4. **Separate epistemic layers** — Observations, transformations, derived measures, statistical inferences, and interpretations must remain distinguishable.
5. **Machine-resolvable provenance** — Every material quantitative result and substantive factual claim must be traceable to its source and transformation history.
6. **Visible uncertainty** — Missingness, ambiguity, model error, classifier uncertainty, sampling limitations, and incompatible measures may not be silently collapsed into certainty.
7. **No silent imputation** — Missing data may be imputed only under an explicit protocol that records the method and sensitivity analysis.
8. **Preregister confirmatory work** — Confirmatory analyses require a frozen, versioned protocol before access to the relevant sealed outcome data.
9. **Separate exploratory and confirmatory analyses** — Exploratory findings may generate hypotheses but may not be represented as preregistered confirmation.
10. **Preserve prior analyses** — Methodological changes after outcome exposure require a new protocol/version; prior analyses and their results remain reproducible.
11. **Independent verification of agent-generated methodology** — An LLM may diagnose, propose, or critique methodology, but material methodological changes require independent evaluation against explicit criteria.
12. **Null and negative findings are first-class results** — They may not be suppressed because they weaken a narrative.
13. **Prefer validated external constructs** — Existing validated instruments and stable survey measures should be preferred over newly invented proxies when they answer the same question.
14. **Multiple independent sensors for broad claims** — Strong claims about cultural or psychological change should not rest on a single platform or dataset when independent evidence can reasonably be obtained.
15. **Alternative explanations are mandatory** — Important apparent trends must be tested against plausible non-awakening explanations such as cohort change, economic development, education, platform composition, terminology drift, moderation, internet adoption, and geopolitical shocks.
16. **Reproducibility outranks persuasion** — The system should prefer a weaker but reproducible conclusion over a stronger but opaque one.
17. **No single Awakening Index by default** — A composite index may be introduced only after statistical and conceptual evidence justifies combining its components.
18. **Fresh-context challenge at major milestones** — Major research conclusions require an independent adversarial review that initially does not inherit prior interpretive conclusions.
19. **Human authority over the constitution** — Autonomous harness evolution may not modify this constitution. Changes require explicit human authorization and versioned history.
20. **The metaphysical claim remains outside direct measurement** — The project may measure observable psychological, behavioral, cultural, and linguistic correlates; it must not present those measurements as direct proof of a metaphysical change in consciousness.

## Authority boundary

| Artifact | Autonomous agent authority |
| --- | --- |
| Implementation code | May change with tests/review |
| Working heuristic | May propose; promote only through evals |
| Harness behavior | May propose; promote only through gated regression + holdout evaluation |
| Exploratory protocol | May create and version |
| Frozen confirmatory protocol | Execute only; never mutate in place |
| Research ontology after outcome exposure | New version only, with explicit disclosure |
| Research Constitution | Human-controlled only |
