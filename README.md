# Awakening Tracker

**Status: research-harness design / early-stage prototype. Not a published study or validated measurement system.**

Awakening Tracker explores a difficult empirical question: are measurable psychological, behavioral, and cultural indicators sometimes associated with spiritual awakening changing over time?

The starting point is *not* that an awakening is happening. The project is designed to investigate the question while giving null results, alternative explanations, and counterevidence the same weight as supportive findings.

## Research approach

The planned research harness emphasizes:

- **Provenance:** traceable sources, transformations, versions, and reproducible results.
- **Preregistration:** a clear distinction between exploratory work and frozen confirmatory analyses.
- **Adversarial evaluation:** active testing of alternative explanations and independent challenges to major conclusions.
- **Human-controlled guardrails:** automated agents may propose work but cannot silently rewrite the underlying research principles.
- **Measurement humility:** observable proxies are not treated as direct measurements of metaphysical claims.

See [RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION.md](RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION.md) for the complete scientific and governance requirements, and [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) for project context.

## Current state

The repository currently focuses on the research design, governance documents, and agent workflow scaffolding. It does **not** yet provide independently validated empirical findings or a production analytics platform.

## Why this is a software project

The engineering challenge is to make AI-assisted research **auditable rather than merely persuasive**: explicit data contracts, reproducible pipelines, evaluation gates, and a separation between generated interpretations and the evidence underlying them.
